<h1>Resource Management Practice</h1>
<p>For the next 30 seconds, only the resource management task will be active.</p>
<p>Num Keys 1-7 control a variety of tanks. Fuel flows in the direction of the arrow.</p>
<p>The top tanks are your flight tanks and should be kept within the black markers.</p>
