<h1>Communications Practice</h1>
<p>For the next 30 seconds, only the communications task will be active.</p>
<p>You can change which radio (up/down) and the frequency (left/right) with the arrow keys.</p>
<p>Listen for your callsign, select the correct radio, tune it to the announced frequency.</p>
<p>Once you have reached your input, press <em>ENTER</em>.</p>
<p>Do not respond to a different call sign.</p>
